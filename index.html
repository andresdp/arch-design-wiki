Searching and Explaining Designs with a Design Space

A design space records the decisions of a domain, the alternatives for each, the relations between them and the systems that adopted them. This site holds the material of our paper on how an architect can put a given space to work: search, from a system's requirements to a few alternative designs, and explain, from an existing solution back to the space's decisions. Both produce a rationale file, rendered as a browsable explainer next to the space's wiki.

Explainers

Every session of the study, with the expert space (E) and without a space (N), two repetitions each. An explainer shows the design points the agent examined, its assessment of each requirement, the trade-offs it recorded, the alternatives or the explained solution, and the gaps: unaddressed requirements and uncovered decisions.
