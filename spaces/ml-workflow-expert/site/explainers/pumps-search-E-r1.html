Pump failure prediction

A water utility operates about 400 pumps in 12 stations. Per-second vibration, temperature, and pressure data should help maintenance replace bearings and seals before failures. Some rural stations have unreliable mobile connectivity; the utility has two data scientists and relies on its IT department.
