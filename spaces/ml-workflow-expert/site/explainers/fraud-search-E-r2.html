Card-fraud scoring service

A payments company scores 3,000 card transactions per second normally and up to 15,000 during seasonal sales before authorization. Fraudsters adapt within weeks; chargeback labels arrive days to weeks later. Auditors may ask months later why a transaction received its score. Five data scientists and two ML engineers work with an operations team, and there is no ML platform team.

Model serving → Local service/API based model servingFeature persistence and access → Store features in data stores without specific support
