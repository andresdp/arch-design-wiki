Card-fraud scoring service

A payments company scores every card transaction for fraud before authorization, at up to 15,000 transactions per second during seasonal peaks. Fraud patterns change within weeks, confirmed chargeback labels arrive days to weeks later, and auditors may ask months later why a score was produced. Seven ML staff work with operations and no dedicated ML platform team.

The solution

S · Fraud scoring as built

The solution uses streaming recent-activity features and synchronous model-service scoring, records score and training provenance, and retrains nightly with a human-reviewed shadow period.

A scheduled MLOps orchestrator performs batch training, versions data, deploys multiple model versions, and tracks production behavior, with humans adjusting models when needed.

ADR1 · Build the authorization-time scoring path around live activity R1Q1

Context. Each authorization needs a score using recent card activity, with a 30 ms p99 target at 15,000 transactions per second. The solution streams transactions, updates aggregates in real time and waits synchronously for an online model service.

Decision. Streamed features and synchronous model microservice.
