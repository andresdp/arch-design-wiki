# About this design space

## What a design space is

A **design space** organizes the design decisions of a domain. Each **dimension** is one decision point, phrased as a question a designer must answer. Its **values** are the alternative answers. **Relations** link dimensions that interact (*requires*, *affects*, *constrains*, *co-occurs with*; see [Relations between dimensions](/relations.md)). A **design point** chooses one value for some dimensions; a **system** occupies the point its choices make.

## Where this one comes from

This design space was imported from a **decision model written by experts**: its decisions become dimensions, their options become values, and the links between decisions become relations. Its values carry no passages from documents: they are cited by their identifiers.
